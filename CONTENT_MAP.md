Sets accepts bounded finite expressions and set-builder predicates, with Venn/Euler, sieve, power-set and ordered-pair/function views. Logic accepts up to four variables and sixteen AST nodes, including arguments with up to three premises. It visualizes exact truth tables, the selected world's gates and counter-worlds; Shape groups by the first two variables while retaining all assignments in labels. Six seeded proof families check the exact marked world set and classification. Curated Python/SQL never executes learner input. The equation workspace supports bounded real curves, implicit planar relations, height surfaces, constants and up to four parameters. It provides numerical views, not a general symbolic solver. The separate Functions teaching lab supplies signed Riemann rectangles, quadratic factoring/square/formula methods and polynomial derivative/integral explanations. Philosophy starts empty so Airator can author the lessons; private reflections are never exported with public lessons.

Summation accepts up to twelve nonnegative quadratic terms or a 4×4 double grid with inclusive bounds. Its live Hopper collects contributions, paired terms expose Gauss's trick, and grid methods change the actual visit order. Small signed integer datasets unfold into exact equal-weight means and population variance, with a standard-deviation ring labelled as an approximation. Six proof families validate the actual term values, included set, mean pin and claim; curated Python, R and SQL preserve the same definitions.

Matrices accepts closed operations on 2×2/3×3 integer matrices: transformation, addition, transpose, multiplication, determinant, 2×2 inverse and Ax=b. Both renderers use genuinely transformed vertices, basis endpoints and lines/planes. Exact elimination distinguishes unique, infinite and inconsistent systems; real eigenspaces are approximate visual annotations. Six proof families validate entered cells or basis coordinates, with a three-part determinant/construction/code Boss. Full bounded context restores offline from notelets.

Functions accepts a closed teaching grammar of degree≤3 integer polynomials, sine/exponential curves, quadratic root equations, polynomial derivatives and signed definite integrals, plus quadratic height surfaces. A trace ball, tangent, parameter controls, touch pinch/wheel zoom, rectangles and a movable surface slice share their geometry in SVG and Three. Finite midpoint sums are labelled approximations, polynomial integrals remain exact, and the square method uses signed strips/corner tiles. Five proof families check the actual construction; three Boss connections, Echoes and complete offline context pass desktop/touch acceptance. Broader equations open in the existing numeric workspace.
